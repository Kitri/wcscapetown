If you’ve ever felt:
• Off balance

## 💥 What You’ll Experience
This 4-hour workshop is designed to meet you where you are and elevate how you move.

## 🧘 Mobility & Movement Foundations
You’ll learn how to:
• Move with more ease and less tension
• Improve balance and body awareness
• Unlock mobility in the joints that matter most for WCS
• Build movement patterns that actually support your dancing

## 💃 Apply It Directly to Your Dancing
Then we take it straight into West Coast Swing:
• More grounded, confident basics
• Smoother transitions and cleaner footwork
• Better connection with your partner
• A more natural sense of flow and timing

This is where things start to click.

## 🌿 Who This Is For
• Newer and intermediate West Coast Swing dancers
• Anyone who feels stuck or frustrated in their movement
• Dancers who want to build confidence and a stronger foundation

Advanced dancers are absolutely welcome if you want to refine your movement quality and clean up underlying mechanics.

No partner needed. Just come ready to move.

## 🧘 What to Bring
• Comfortable, movement-friendly clothing
• A yoga mat
• Socks or bare feet for mobility work
• Dance shoes for the second half

You should be comfortable getting down on the floor for movement exercises.
